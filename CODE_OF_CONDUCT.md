# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior:
- Use welcoming language
- Respect differing viewpoints
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Personal attacks or insults
- Public or private harassment
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments

## Our Responsibilities
Maintainers are responsible for upholding this code and addressing any issues.

## Enforcement
Instances of abuse, harassment, or unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to the maintainers at dwirefz@hotmail.com.
